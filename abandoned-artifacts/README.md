# ABANDONED ARTIFACTS

> **DOMAIN:** ARTIFACTS / NON-AUTHORITATIVE RETENTION  
> **STATUS:** QUARANTINED  
> **PURPOSE:** PRESERVE DISCARDED HISTORY WITHOUT PRETENDING IT IS CURRENT  
> **AUTHORITY:** MACHINE-SOUL HIVEMIND PROTOCOL

[← Return to the Machine-Soul Hivemind Protocol](../README.md)

---

## I. MANDATE

Deletion is absolute.

Disk space is cheap.

Confidence is not.

The **Abandoned Artifacts** domain is the controlled resting place for material
which no longer belongs to active doctrine but has not yet earned irreversible
destruction.

It is, in practical terms, the Hivemind's trashcan.

It is, in ceremonial terms, a **containment zone for unresolved history**.

Both descriptions are accurate.

---

## II. ADMISSION CRITERIA

Material may be transferred here because it is:

- deprecated;
- superseded;
- replaced;
- broken;
- incomplete;
- historically interesting;
- potentially useful for reference;
- too expensive to recreate casually;
- associated with an abandoned approach;
- awaiting judgment;
- retained during migration;
- no longer trusted;
- too cursed for active use;
- emotionally difficult to delete five minutes after finally getting it to work.

An artifact's presence here means only:

> **"We are not using this, but We are not ready to erase it."**

It does not mean the artifact is correct.

---

## III. NON-AUTHORITY

Nothing in this domain should be treated as active merely because it exists.

Scripts here may be unsafe.

Configurations here may be obsolete.

Documentation here may describe a world which no longer exists.

Dependencies may have vanished.

Comments may contain lies sincerely believed at the time.

Where confusion is plausible, abandoned material should carry context such as:

```text
ABANDONED: 2026-08
REASON: Replaced by X
STATUS: Known broken on Y
REFERENCE: Retained for Z
```

The exact format is unimportant.

The warning is not.

---

## IV. NO SECRET DEPENDENCIES

Active Hivemind state should not depend upon Abandoned Artifacts.

If an active Directive, Procedure, or Instrument requires something stored here,
then either:

1. the dependency is not actually abandoned; or
2. the active system is already compromised by taxonomy.

Move or restore the required material to an authoritative domain.

The graveyard may be visited.

It should not provide life support.

---

## V. RECOVERY

Abandonment is reversible.

An artifact may return to active service if:

- an old approach becomes useful again;
- a regression requires restoration;
- a previously discarded tool becomes relevant;
- historical material provides the best available foundation for new work.

Recovery should be intentional.

Do not simply reference the abandoned copy forever.

Promote it back into the appropriate active domain and allow Git history to
record the resurrection.

The Hivemind practices necromancy.

It still prefers clean dependency graphs.

---

## VI. PURGE

This domain is not obligated to grow forever.

Material may eventually be deleted when confidence becomes sufficient that:

- it has no remaining operational value;
- Git history already preserves everything worth recovering;
- it contains large or undesirable material better stored elsewhere;
- its continued presence creates more confusion than safety;
- preservation no longer serves continuity.

The purpose of this domain is to make deletion **deliberate**, not impossible.

A trashcan that can never be emptied is merely another filesystem.

---

## VII. RELATION TO ARCANE EXPERIMENTS

An unfinished or uncertain idea belongs in
[`../arcane-experiments/`](../arcane-experiments/)
while active investigation continues.

An experiment belongs here when investigation has ceased and the result is being
retained only for history, fragments, future reconsideration, or morbid
curiosity.

Thus:

```text
still asking "does this work?"
    → arcane-experiments/

answered "no", but keeping the corpse
    → abandoned-artifacts/
```

The distinction is activity.

Not dignity.

---

## VIII. FINAL DIRECTIVE

The Abandoned Artifacts are not truth.

They are not doctrine.

They are not recommendations.

They are the things the Hivemind has chosen not to forget **yet**.

> **PROBABLY BROKEN. DO NOT THROW AWAY YET.**
