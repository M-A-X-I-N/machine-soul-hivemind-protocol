# ARCANE EXPERIMENTS

> **DOMAIN:** EXPERIMENTATION / UNSANCTIONED DEVELOPMENT  
> **STATUS:** UNSTABLE BY DEFINITION  
> **PURPOSE:** DISCOVER WHAT THE HIVEMIND MAY SAFELY BELIEVE OR BECOME NEXT  
> **AUTHORITY:** PROVISIONAL

[← Return to the Machine-Soul Hivemind Protocol](../README.md)

---

## I. MANDATE

All sanctioned doctrine was once questionable.

All reliable instruments were once untested.

All sufficiently obscure Windows configuration eventually becomes experimental
physics.

The **Arcane Experiments** domain contains work which is actively being
investigated but has not yet earned permanent classification elsewhere in the
Hivemind.

It is the laboratory.

It is the proving ground.

It is where ideas are permitted to be wrong before being required to become
useful.

---

## II. ACCEPTABLE ABOMINATIONS

This domain may contain:

- prototypes;
- proof-of-concept scripts;
- unfinished tools;
- reverse-engineering work;
- registry experiments;
- exploratory configuration;
- diagnostic hooks;
- temporary test harnesses;
- API probes;
- format investigations;
- throwaway code that has not yet admitted it will become permanent;
- dangerous ideas;
- suspicious ideas;
- ideas whose danger cannot yet be distinguished from their usefulness;
- things that technically worked but caused immediate philosophical concern.

Experimentation need not be clean.

It should, however, remain comprehensible enough that the experiment can later
be judged.

---

## III. ISOLATION

Experimental material should not silently become a production dependency.

The Hivemind may test unstable work.

It should not forget that the work is unstable.

When an experiment can alter important system state, appropriate caution may
include:

- backups;
- explicit privilege requirements;
- reversal instructions;
- test machines;
- disposable state;
- comments describing expected effects;
- logging;
- before/after observations;
- avoiding destructive operations unless destruction is itself the experiment.

The purpose of an experiment is to acquire knowledge.

Destroying the evidence before learning anything is merely chaos.

---

## IV. RECORD THE QUESTION

A useful experiment should make clear what uncertainty it exists to resolve.

This may be no more elaborate than:

```text
Question:
Does DWM actually honor NONCLIENTMETRICS.lfFaceName for native captions?

Observed:
Metrics change. Typeface does not.

Next:
Probe DirectWrite calls inside DWM.
```

The format is optional.

The principle is not.

An experiment without a remembered question quickly becomes a directory full of
files named `test2-final-real.ps1`.

Such states are hostile to continuity.

---

## V. PROMOTION PATHS

Successful experiments should eventually leave this domain.

Their destination depends upon what they become:

```text
arcane-experiments/
        │
        ├── becomes host setup machinery
        │       └──> ../annexation-procedures/
        │
        ├── becomes canonical configuration
        │       └──> ../assimilation-directives/
        │
        ├── becomes a reusable tool
        │       └──> ../accumulated-instruments/
        │
        ├── produces retained knowledge
        │       └──> ../acquired-intelligence/
        │
        ├── produces a reusable static resource
        │       └──> ../assembled-assets/
        │
        └── dies but remains interesting
                └──> ../abandoned-artifacts/
```

Experimentation is a transitional state.

Permanent residency should be reserved for work that is genuinely still alive.

---

## VI. FAILURE

Experiments are permitted to fail.

This is not merely tolerated.

It is expected.

A failed experiment may still produce:

- eliminated hypotheses;
- diagnostics;
- knowledge;
- reusable fragments;
- evidence of platform behavior;
- proof that an elegant approach is impossible;
- a warning to future manifestations.

If useful knowledge was gained, record it under
[`../acquired-intelligence/`](../acquired-intelligence/).

If the corpse remains useful, move it to
[`../abandoned-artifacts/`](../abandoned-artifacts/).

Repeatedly performing the same forgotten failure is not experimentation.

It is ritualized amnesia.

---

## VII. WHEN AN EXPERIMENT BECOMES REAL

Promotion should occur when the primary uncertainty has been resolved and the
result has acquired a stable purpose.

Ask:

> Is this now how a host should be configured?

Promote it to Assimilation Directives.

> Is this now part of acquiring a host?

Promote it to Annexation Procedures.

> Is this now a reusable capability?

Promote it to Accumulated Instruments.

> Was the important result the knowledge rather than the code?

Preserve the result as Acquired Intelligence.

> Did we merely discover a new and exciting way to make things worse?

Contain the remains appropriately.

---

## VIII. FINAL DIRECTIVE

The Hivemind advances by allowing temporary heresy under controlled conditions.

Question assumptions.

Probe undocumented behavior.

Reverse-engineer the lie.

Test the impossible solution.

Record the result.

Then either canonize the discovery or bury the corpse.

> **EXPERIMENT FREELY. PROMOTE DELIBERATELY. FORGET NOTHING USEFUL.**
