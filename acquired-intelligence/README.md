# ACQUIRED INTELLIGENCE

> **DOMAIN:** INTELLIGENCE / RETAINED KNOWLEDGE  
> **STATUS:** ACCUMULATING  
> **PURPOSE:** PREVENT THE HIVEMIND FROM PAYING TWICE FOR THE SAME LESSON  
> **AUTHORITY:** MACHINE-SOUL HIVEMIND PROTOCOL

[← Return to the Machine-Soul Hivemind Protocol](../README.md)

---

## I. MANDATE

Not all valuable state is executable.

Some of the most expensive knowledge begins with:

> "Oh. So *that's* what Windows is doing."

The **Acquired Intelligence** domain preserves observations, discoveries,
references, useful links, technical notes, remembered decisions, and other
knowledge which does not belong naturally in configuration or code.

It is the long-term memory of the Hivemind.

The purpose is simple:

> **A lesson already purchased with time shall not be purchased again through
> forgetfulness.**

---

## II. ACCEPTABLE INTELLIGENCE

Material may include:

- notes on obscure platform behavior;
- useful repositories;
- useful software and utilities;
- documentation links worth finding again;
- command snippets accompanied by context;
- explanations of non-obvious configuration decisions;
- reverse-engineering findings;
- operating-system quirks;
- compatibility observations;
- troubleshooting records;
- architectural notes;
- reminders of things that are obvious only immediately after learning them;
- lists of resources for future work;
- explanations of why a tempting approach failed;
- references whose loss would force future archaeology.

A note need not be profound.

If future-you is plausibly capable of asking the same question again, the
Hivemind has a reason to remember the answer.

---

## III. INTELLIGENCE IS NOT DOCTRINE

This domain explains reality.

It does not automatically define desired state.

A note saying:

> Windows reads this setting from an unexpected registry location.

belongs here.

The script or configuration which intentionally sets that value may belong in
[`../accumulated-instruments/`](../accumulated-instruments/)
or
[`../assimilation-directives/`](../assimilation-directives/).

The intelligence explains **why**.

The instrument performs **how**.

The directive declares **what shall be**.

These may reference one another.

They should not be confused merely because they were discovered during the same
afternoon of suffering.

---

## IV. PROVENANCE AND CONFIDENCE

Useful intelligence should preserve enough context to distinguish:

```text
observed directly
documented officially
reported externally
suspected
historical
obsolete
unknown
```

Formal citation bureaucracy is not required for every note.

But when a claim came from an external source, preserving the source is often
valuable—especially when the source is obscure, surprising, likely to vanish,
or contradicts what sane software would be expected to do.

A sentence such as:

> "Apparently this only works after restart."

is useful.

A sentence such as:

> "Apparently this only works after restart; confirmed on Spaceship,
> Windows 11, after testing X and Y."

is future-proofed intelligence.

Precision may be accumulated incrementally.

---

## V. USEFUL REFERENCES

Lists of valuable external material explicitly belong here.

Examples:

```text
useful-repositories.md
useful-software.md
windows/
powershell/
shells/
terminals/
hardware/
development/
references/
```

The Hivemind is not expected to reproduce the entire public Internet.

It is expected to remember **which fragments of the Internet were actually
worth returning to**.

Where links are especially valuable, a short annotation explaining *why* they
matter is preferable to an unexplained graveyard of URLs.

A link without remembered relevance is merely a future clicking exercise.

---

## VI. SCAR TISSUE

A recurring class of intelligence consists of behavior which was discovered
only because something failed, lied, reset itself, ignored documentation, or
behaved correctly for reasons hostile to intuition.

Examples include:

> Application Y silently ignores setting Z unless ritual A occurs first.

> The UI exposes this value but does not actually control the subsystem that
> consumes it.

> The documented API reports one font while the compositor uses another.

> This workaround is ugly, but the elegant approaches were tested and do not
> work.

> Microsoft has committed another act against determinism.

These notes are not clutter.

They are **scar tissue**.

Scar tissue is evidence that the organism survived contact with reality.

---

## VII. INTELLIGENCE FROM EXPERIMENTATION

Arcane Experiments frequently generate useful knowledge even when the experiment
itself fails.

When this occurs, preserve the conclusion here before disposing of or archiving
the experimental machinery.

A failed experiment which eliminates three plausible explanations has produced
intelligence.

A failed experiment which is forgotten has produced only wasted time.

See [`../arcane-experiments/`](../arcane-experiments/).

---

## VIII. FINAL DIRECTIVE

The Hivemind does not require omniscience.

It requires continuity.

Write down the strange thing.

Save the useful repository.

Record why the workaround exists.

Explain the decision while the reason still seems impossible to forget.

It will become possible.

> **MEMORY IS CHEAPER THAN REDISCOVERY.**
